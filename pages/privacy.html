Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 1, 2026

The Great Outdoors ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with a great experience:

  • Contact form submissions — name, email address, and message when you contact us voluntarily.
  • Reviews you submit — trail reviews and photos you choose to post are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and are not transmitted to a server.
  • Usage data — standard web analytics data such as pages visited and time on site (no personally identifiable information).

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through contact forms is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This site uses only essential browser storage (localStorage) to save your preferences such as trail reviews you've written. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external sites such as the Washington Trails Association (wta.org) and Leave No Trace (lnt.org). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies when you visit them.

5. Images & Media

Trail and news images are sourced from Unsplash and the Washington Trails Association. These services may have their own data collection practices. Photos you upload as part of a trail review are stored only in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information submitted through our contact forms. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
