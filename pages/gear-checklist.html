Seasonal Tips

🌸

Spring (Mar–May)

  • Expect muddy, saturated trails — gaiters recommended
  • Snow still present above 3,000 ft — check conditions
  • Wildflower blooms peak in May on lowland trails
  • Waterproof boots essential
☀️

Summer (Jun–Aug)

  • Best conditions for alpine trails — limited snow
  • Start early to beat afternoon heat and crowds
  • Carry extra water — streams may be dry
  • Check for fire closures and smoke advisories
🍂

Fall (Sep–Nov)

  • Stunning foliage — best colors in late September
  • Rain returns — pack rain gear always
  • Shorter days — bring a headlamp even for day hikes
  • Hunting season in some areas — wear bright colors
❄️

Winter (Dec–Feb)

  • Many alpine trails closed — stick to lowland routes
  • Microspikes required on most trails
  • Shorter daylight — plan for 4–5 hours of light max
  • Franklin Falls and Twin Falls stay open year-round
